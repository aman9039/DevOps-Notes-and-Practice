Q1. What is a Namespace?
Answer: A Namespace logically separates Kubernetes resources inside the same cluster.

Q2. Why use Namespaces?
Answer: To organize applications and isolate development, testing, and production resources.

Q.3 What is a Deployment?
Answer: A Deployment manages Pods and ensures the desired number of replicas are always running.

Q.4 Why use replicas?
Answer: To provide high availability and fault tolerance.

Q.5 What is a selector?
Answer: A selector matches Pods using labels so the Deployment knows which Pods it manages.

Q.6 What is a Service?
Answer: A Service provides a stable network endpoint to access one or more Pods.

Q.7 Why not use Pod IPs?
Answer: Pod IPs are temporary and can change when Pods are recreated.

Q.8 What does ClusterIP mean?
Answer: The Service is available only inside the Kubernetes cluster

Q.9 Why do we create a Service for the frontend?
Answer: To provide a stable endpoint for accessing frontend Pods.

Q.10 Can a Service create Pods?
Answer: No. A Service only routes traffic to existing Pods.

Q.11 What is an Ingress?
Answer: An Ingress routes external HTTP/HTTPS traffic to Kubernetes Services.

Q.12 Why use Ingress?
Answer: 
Single entry point
Path-based routing
Host-based routing
TLS/HTTPS support

Q.13 Does Ingress send traffic directly to Pods?
Answer: ❌ No.
It sends traffic to a Service, and the Service forwards it to the Pods.
