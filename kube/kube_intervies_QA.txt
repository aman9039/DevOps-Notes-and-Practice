Q1. What is a Namespace?
Answer: A Namespace logically separates Kubernetes resources inside the same cluster.

Q2. Why use Namespaces?
Answer: To organize applications and isolate development, testing, and production resources.

Q.3 What is a Deployment?
Answer: A Deployment manages Pods and ensures the desired number of replicas are always running.

Q.4 Why use replicas?
Answer: To provide high availability and fault tolerance.

Q.5 What is a selector?
Answer: A selector matches Pods using labels so the Deployment knows which Pods it manages.
